## 0.1.0 Developer preview

- Vue and NativeScript-Vue share semantic colors and a white light-mode canvas.
- The Web catalog includes interactive examples for inputs, overlays, tables, charts, and conversation interfaces.
- Native previews can be built for iOS and Android.
- The website includes English and Simplified Chinese pages, live templates, and a theme lab.
- Page bodies, template shells, and large previews render directly on the canvas. The design rules are available in Markdown, through the CLI, and from project agent instructions. [Design rules](/docs/design-rules/)
